## 1. Using OpenCaller

- You need an Android phone and, for online features, an account tied to a phone number you control (we check it
  with a code).
- You must be at least [13/16] years old, or have a parent's or guardian's permission where the law allows that.
- Keep your phone and your account to yourself. You are responsible for what is done with your account.

## 2. What OpenCaller does, and what it can't promise

- Caller ID, spam labels, the scam feed, OTP warnings and the voice scanner are **warnings, not verdicts**. They come
  from reports by other users, public registries and automatic checks, and they can be wrong both ways: a real
  caller can be labelled spam, and a scammer can look harmless. The voice scanner in particular can mistake a real
  voice for a generated one, especially on older mobile networks.
- Blocking rules decide on your phone and fail open: if something goes wrong, the call rings. OpenCaller never
  blocks emergency numbers or your outgoing calls.
- Online features need our server and the phone network. We try to keep them running but don't promise they always
  will.
- Never rely on OpenCaller alone to decide whether to trust a caller. If in doubt, hang up and call back on a number
  you know.

## 3. Reports, names and other content you add

- When you report a number, label it, write a comment, add a profile name, picture or video, or claim a business, you
  must tell the truth and have the right to share it. Don't report numbers to harass someone, don't impersonate
  anyone, and don't upload anything illegal, hateful or sexual.
- You give us permission to store, process and show that content as the app describes (for example, a report adds to
  a number's spam score that other users see). You can delete your profile content and your account at any time.
- We may hide or remove content and suspend accounts that break these terms, and we may limit how many reports and
  searches an account can make.
- Anyone who thinks their number is labelled wrongly can dispute it in the app or ask for it to be unlisted at
  [server URL]/unlist.

## 4. Calls, recordings and the call assistant

- **Recording:** recording a call can be illegal without everyone's consent. OpenCaller plays a beep and an
  announcement, but **you** must make sure you are allowed to record, tell the other person where the law requires
  it, and use recordings lawfully. Recording is offered only in [approved countries].
- **Call assistant:** calls you forward to your assistant number are answered by an automated assistant that says it
  is one. You are responsible for forwarding only your own calls. Your carrier may charge for forwarded calls.
- **Voice scan:** the other person hears a beep. Use it only to protect yourself from fraud.
- App calls and Flash messages are for people who know each other. Don't use them for marketing or spam.
