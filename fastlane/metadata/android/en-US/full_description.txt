OpenCaller is an open-source caller ID, spam blocker, phone app and SMS app. It is built so that your address book, call history and messages never leave your phone.

<b>Block spam calls</b>
* Block, silence or allow numbers, prefixes, countries and patterns, hidden numbers and numbers not in your contacts.
* A community spam list, updated daily, catches known spam callers. Your contacts always ring; verified businesses are kept off the spam list and ring even when you block non-contacts.
* Decisions are made on your phone in milliseconds. If something goes wrong, the call rings: OpenCaller never blocks emergency numbers or outgoing calls.

<b>Know who is calling</b>
* A caller card shows the name, where that name comes from (your contact, the number's owner, a verified business, an official sender registry) and any spam reports.
* Names never come from other people's address books.
* Verified businesses show their logo and why they call.

<b>Phone app and dialer</b>
* Full in-call screen, call waiting, hold, merge, Bluetooth, dual SIM.
* Dial pad with suggestions from your contacts, a call log with filters, and a contacts editor.

<b>Messages</b>
* SMS and MMS, sorted on the phone into Personal, Codes, Transactions, Bills, Deliveries, Promotions and Spam.
* One-time codes get their own notification with Copy, never shown on the lock screen.
* If a code arrives while a stranger is on the phone with you, OpenCaller warns you not to share it.
* Links that imitate well-known brands get a warning.

<b>Family protection</b>
* Start a group of up to 5 people and invite them with a code. Nobody can be added without their own consent on their own phone.
* Each member chooses what their admin sees: a message when a one-time code arrives during a call with a stranger, and once a day how many calls and spam messages OpenCaller stopped. Never who called or texted, never numbers or messages.
* Scam alerts: current scams in your country, written by OpenCaller's editors, in the Protection tab.

<b>Calls and messages between OpenCaller users</b>
* Flash messages ("Call me", "Running late") and a short video caller ID, both optional. App-to-app calls over the internet are in the GitHub and Google Play builds, not in this one (they need prebuilt calling libraries).

<b>Privacy</b>
* No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
* Signing in is optional. Without an account, blocking works with your own rules.
* Live lookups send only a short, anonymous hash prefix of the number, never the number itself.
* A report you choose to send contains the reported number, a label and your optional comment, nothing about your contacts.

<b>Businesses</b>
Businesses can claim their number by proving they control it and sending documents for review. Verified businesses get a web dashboard to keep their hours and call reasons up to date and see how often their numbers are searched and reported, never who searched or reported.

This build has no Google services. Online features need an OpenCaller server; without one, blocking, the dialer and messages work on the phone.

OpenCaller is free software under the GPL-3.0.
